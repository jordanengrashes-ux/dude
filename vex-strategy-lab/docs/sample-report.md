## Limitations that affect these numbers

- Rules were taken from search-engine extracts of the official manual (v2.1) and Q&A; the official sites could not be opened from the build environment. See the Rules tab / `src/rules/ruleset.ts` for per-rule provenance.
- Field element positions follow the published dimensions where found and a symmetric layout otherwise (marked as assumptions in `src/field/geometry.ts`).
- The robot is the default configuration (16 × 16 in, 4WD, rack-and-pinion); unknown dimensions, gearing, masses and friction are assumptions, editable in the Robot tab.
- Other robots are modeled in the planner as predicted events; in the physics runs they react only as their scripts allow. Interaction (blocking, pushing, contested objects) is simulated physically but not anticipated beyond the prediction.
- Physics clutter (plowed or wedged objects) is genuine simulation behavior and is the main source of the model-vs-physics gap.
